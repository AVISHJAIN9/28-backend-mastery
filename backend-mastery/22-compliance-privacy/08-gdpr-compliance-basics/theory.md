# Theory – GDPR Compliance Basics

## What is the GDPR?
The **General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679** is the EU's primary data protection law. It applies to any organisation that processes personal data of EU/EEA residents, regardless of where the organisation is located.

## The 7 Principles (Article 5)
1. **Lawfulness, fairness, transparency** – have a legal basis; be honest with people
2. **Purpose limitation** – only use data for what you said you would
3. **Data minimisation** – collect only what you need
4. **Accuracy** – keep data up to date
5. **Storage limitation** – don't keep data longer than necessary
6. **Integrity and confidentiality** – secure the data
7. **Accountability** – be able to demonstrate compliance

## The 6 Lawful Bases (Article 6)
| Basis | Article | Notes |
|-------|---------|-------|
| Consent | 6(1)(a) | Must be freely given, specific, informed, unambiguous |
| Contract | 6(1)(b) | Must be necessary for contract performance |
| Legal obligation | 6(1)(c) | Specific legal duty in EU/member state law |
| Vital interests | 6(1)(d) | Life or death only |
| Public task | 6(1)(e) | Public authorities and bodies |
| Legitimate interests | 6(1)(f) | Requires a Legitimate Interests Assessment (LIA) |

## Data Subject Rights (Articles 12–22)
| Right | Article | Timeframe |
|-------|---------|-----------|
| Right to be informed | 13-14 | At point of collection |
| Right of access | 15 | 1 month |
| Right to rectification | 16 | 1 month |
| Right to erasure | 17 | 1 month |
| Right to restrict processing | 18 | Without undue delay |
| Right to data portability | 20 | 1 month |
| Right to object | 21 | Immediately (marketing); 1 month (other) |
| Rights re automated decisions | 22 | 1 month |

## Key Organisational Requirements
- **Privacy Notice** (Art. 13-14): Tell people what you collect and why
- **ROPA** (Art. 30): Maintain a Record of Processing Activities
- **DPIA** (Art. 35): Required for high-risk processing
- **DPO** (Art. 37-39): Required for public bodies and large-scale monitoring
- **Security** (Art. 32): Appropriate technical and organisational measures
- **Breach Notification** (Art. 33-34): Notify authority within 72 hours

## Penalties (Article 83)
- Tier 1: Up to €10M or 2% of global annual turnover
- Tier 2: Up to €20M or 4% of global annual turnover (most serious violations)

## UK GDPR
Post-Brexit, the UK has its own UK GDPR (almost identical to EU GDPR), enforced by the ICO. Standard Contractual Clauses needed for transfers from UK to non-adequate countries.
