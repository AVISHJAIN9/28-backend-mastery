# Theory – Consent Management

## What is Valid Consent? (GDPR Art. 7)
Consent must be:
- **Freely given** – no bundling or coercion
- **Specific** – one purpose per consent
- **Informed** – user knows what they're agreeing to
- **Unambiguous** – a clear affirmative act (no pre-ticked boxes)
- **Withdrawable** – as easy to withdraw as to give

## What Must You Record?
For each consent event, record:
- Who gave consent (user ID)
- What they consented to (category/purpose)
- When (timestamp)
- How (source: signup form, preference centre, etc.)
- Which version of the privacy notice was shown
- IP address and user agent

## Consent vs. Other Lawful Bases
Consent is just one of six lawful bases under GDPR Art. 6:
1. Consent
2. Contract performance
3. Legal obligation
4. Vital interests
5. Public task
6. Legitimate interests

Only use consent where you genuinely need it. For core service delivery, contract or legitimate interests is usually more appropriate.

## DPDP Act 2023 (India)
The DPDP Act requires "free, specific, informed, unconditional and unambiguous" consent. Consent notices must be available in multiple Indian languages. Consent managers (registered intermediaries) may be used.
