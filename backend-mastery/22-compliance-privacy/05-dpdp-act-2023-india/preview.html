🇮🇳 DPDP Act 2023 – Your Data Rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, you have the following rights:

📋
Access My Data
Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you.
SLA: 30 days
✏️
Correct My Data
Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
SLA: 30 days
🗑️
Erase My Data
Request deletion of your personal data.
SLA: 30 days
📣
File a Grievance
Raise a complaint with our Grievance Officer.
SLA: 7 days
👤
Nominate a Representative
Designate someone to exercise your rights on your behalf.
SLA: 7 days

Grievance Officer: grievance@example.com · Appeals to the Data Protection Board of India
